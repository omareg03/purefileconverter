### Where the "Page with redirect" URLs come from (live `curl -I`)
| URL Google found | Response | Intentional? |
|---|---|---|
| `http://purefileconverter.com/…` | 301 → https (Render edge) | Yes |
| `http(s)://www.purefileconverter.com/…` | 301 → apex https | Yes |
| `https://purefileconverter.com/blog/` (any path + `/`) | **307** → no-slash (Starlette `redirect_slashes`) | Harmless but *temporary* |
| `http://purefileconverter.com/blog/` | 301 → https `/blog/` → 307 → `/blog` | Two-hop chain |

**Verdict:** the email is mostly Google reporting your http/www variants, which is correct behaviour; those URLs are *supposed* to stay out of the index. Your real pages are not affected. The fixes below remove the only weak spots (307 + two-hop chain) and add a test so this never regresses.
