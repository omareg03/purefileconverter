Privacy Policy

At PureFileConverter, your privacy is our priority. Here is how we handle your data:

1. File Deletion

We do not store your files permanently. All uploaded and converted files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers within 1 hour. We do not inspect, copy, or share your files with any third parties.

2. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies to improve your experience. We may also use Google Analytics to understand how our site is used.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to serve ads on our site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
